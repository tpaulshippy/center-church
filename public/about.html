About us

Who we are
& why we're here.

Our mission and our values demonstrate who we are — and flow from what we believe.

Our mission

To make and mature disciples of Jesus Christ

Center Church exists to make and mature disciples of Jesus Christ through biblically-grounded expositional preaching, intentional discipleship, and servant-hearted outreach.

1 · Preaching

We believe the Bible is God's perfect, infallible Word. Because we treasure the Word, we prioritize preaching each Sunday — typically working straight through whole books or large passages over weeks or months.

2 · Discipleship

From the day our hearts are awakened to the grace of God, we press "onward and upward." The local church is the optimal place to grow in our love for Jesus together, day by day, into his likeness.

3 · Outreach

The world around us is dying to meet Jesus. We meet people where they are — often through physical and material needs — with a heart to help them see the spiritual need only Jesus can satisfy.

Our values

What shapes everything we do

Gospel-Centered

It's all about Jesus. From beginning to end, the Bible points to the reality that God became man to save mankind. We preach, sing, pray, and counsel the gospel — and build our church upon it.

Reformed

God is the all-glorious, triune source and end of all things, sovereignly working all things according to his will. His sovereign grace humbles us and fills us with inexpressible gratitude.

Complementarian

God created man and woman in his image with equal value and dignity, appointing differing yet complementary roles — something the church gets to celebrate, contend for, and protect.

Missional

Jesus has called the whole church to be on mission until he returns. Embracing our identity as those sent by Jesus is essential to the Christian life.

Elder-Governed

Jesus Christ reigns as head over the church, and he gives elders to govern and lead under his authority — men qualified by both character and gifting.

Continuationist

To do what Jesus commanded, we need his power. The Holy Spirit empowers us for life and mission and gives supernatural gifts to build up the church, just as in the book of Acts.

Partnerships

We don't do this alone

In the Bible we see the importance of interdependence among God's churches — allowing us to do far more together than we ever could alone.

Center Church is united in fellowship, mission, beliefs, and governance with Sovereign Grace Churches.

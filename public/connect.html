Ministries to help you grow

Small Groups

From the beginning, Christians gathered in smaller groups to fellowship, study the Word, share life, and follow Christ together. Small groups are how we live out the value of family beyond Sunday mornings — we love, laugh, learn, cry, encourage, eat, comfort, and pray for each other. There is always room for someone new.

Explore small groups →

Men's & Women's Ministries

Men and women, created equal in value and purpose, are both called to grow in Jesus and serve his church. Our men's and women's ministries provide Bible studies, prayer groups, service opportunities, and events that keep our focus on Jesus.

See upcoming studies →

Youth Ministry

The Center Church Youth exist to evangelize and disciple junior high and high school students — and to create opportunities for meaningful relationships and connection within the church.

Learn more →

R12 — Singles & Young Adults

Coming of age in 21st-century America is harder than ever. R12 exists to present and cultivate a biblical worldview in a non-threatening, conversational environment — talking about the big ideas everyone is discussing, informed by Scripture.

Learn more →

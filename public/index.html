Center Church at a glance

A community preaching Christ's message of hope

Join our loving community of believers in Gilbert, Arizona — learning to remain fixed on Jesus in all areas of life.

Jesus

The good news of who Jesus is and all he's done is the center of our church. We celebrate this good news every Sunday.

Community

Our small groups are the springboard of community — where we meet to know one another, pray together, and help one another.

Gilbert, AZ

Whether giving back to the community, providing meals, or meeting practical needs, Center Church loves to serve others.

Grace

No matter your religious background or life experience, there is complete forgiveness and acceptance for all who put their faith in Christ.

Sunday mornings

The best place to start? Join us on a Sunday.

  • Arrive — service starts at 10:00 AM; come early for guest parking and a free coffee.
  • Can't make it in person? — we live-stream every Sunday at 10:00 AM on YouTube.
  • Have kids? — we offer classes for children up to fourth grade. Children join us for singing, then are dismissed to classrooms before the sermon.
  • Singing — from hymns to contemporary, we sing biblical truths to Jesus and each other.
  • Preaching — verse-by-verse through books of the Bible, with Jesus at the center.

Rich Richardson: Yeah, they're different.

Now, people say many things when they say double predestination.

I don't hold to double predestination the way most people talk about it. Double predestination, the way most people talk about it, is the teaching that God elects some to receive the gospel and be saved, and he elects others to go to hell. So we see, we clearly see a degree of election all over the Bible. We see it. The Lord chose Israel, not Egypt. The Lord chose Israel, not the Philistines. The Lord chose Israel, not the Mayas, the Incas, and the Aztecs. The Lord chose Israel and not other nations, right? Mm-hmm. And so we also see in Ephesians chapter 1 that the Lord elects before time began.

Now, in election, he actively works grace and favor into the lives of the people. Now, to believe that he would elect people to go to hell would mean that he would actively work rejection and hate toward him into their lives. He doesn't have to actively work that sin into their lives. The people who are, who are not elect, they're not going to be able to point to the Lord and say, it's because you didn't elect me that I'm not saved. What it's going to be is their fault, right? Now, how this works is completely— it's a mystery. It's really difficult, right? It's more of a philosophical argument to say, could If God elects some to heaven, he elects some to hell. Now, he elects the ones he elects to be with him for all time. You know, that does not mitigate.

It doesn't destroy our responsibility. But in election, he doesn't elect to reject in the sense that, okay, I'm going to make sure that this person turns their back on me. I'm going to make sure that this person has no time for me. I mean, to reject, to elect someone to go to hell, it would be that way. I'm going to make sure that this person is evil. I'm going to make sure that this person has all kinds of hatred for his fellow man. And so, that's something that the Lord would never— because he's not the author of evil, he would never do that. What we have, though, is God electing some by grace to be saved, and others who do not choose to bow the knee, they are the ones that end up being— and if they die in their sins, they're apart from God for all eternity.

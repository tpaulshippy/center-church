I pray that you, Jesus, would be the center and the focal point of our church both today and forevermore. And I pray that you would help us as a body together, collectively and individually, to build our lives more and more around you.

Because there is no one worth sacrificing like you. And Jesus, it's in your name we pray. Amen.

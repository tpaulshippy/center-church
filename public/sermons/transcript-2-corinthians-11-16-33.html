We don't know the end from the beginning, but we're grateful you do.

We're grateful that you, though we are weak and though we fail, have seen fit to come and empower us, not despite our weakness, but because of our weakness.

And so, Lord, I pray that you would help us not to hate our weaknesses so much. Help us.

Help us to recognize, wow, where I see a weakness, now I can run to the Lord and ask him to help because it's there. He's going to do work he wouldn't do in my life otherwise.

And so, Lord, we take our failures and our regrets, we take our disappointments and depression, we take our anxieties, and we pile them up upon you and say, Lord, we can't do it. Help.

We trust you. We look to you. We rely on you. We have no one else but you.

Jesus, it's in your name we pray. Amen.

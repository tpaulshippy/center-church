Friends, we have the opportunity to enjoy, the privilege, the exciting, thrilling journey and adventure to live for Him because living for anyone else isn't worth it.

So may we make our continual overriding preoccupation at all things in all times to please Him.

And He stands ready to help. May the grace of our Lord Jesus be with us all. You're dismissed. We'll see you next Sunday. Thank you.

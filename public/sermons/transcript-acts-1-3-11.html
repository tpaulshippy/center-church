I pray that you would help us to hear of the reports of your people going forth and taking risks, your people going forth, whether it's around the corner or around the world, to take risks so that they might be ones who share the gospel of Jesus Christ and tell of this new advocate in heaven that stands for them and can be their Savior, Lord.

For this, we're utterly reliant upon you. Holy Spirit, I pray that you would fill us and empower us for this work. And it's in your mighty name we pray. Amen.

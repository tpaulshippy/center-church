responsibilities of those who open up God's Word to preach involves preaching the Bible with a focus on exposing the fallen condition of our hearts. My task today is to help us all see how Acts 11 verses 1 through 18 exposes us. And with the Lord's help, we will see how God wants to transform us as His people. What this passage does is it shows us our proclivity to criticize what God might be doing in other people rather than celebrate the grace of God at work in people's lives. It's a resistance that doesn't come from outside of the church. It comes from within.

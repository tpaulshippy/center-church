But unlike Odysseus, who died and remained dead, Jesus died and rose to life. Odysseus lost his throne. Jesus ascended to his rightful place at the right hand of God, sitting on his eternal throne. Odysseus lost his opportunity to live happily ever after with his bride, but Jesus is coming back one day for his bride, and he will find us in purity and in splendor. Friends, that's the good news that we have to share with people. That's the good news that we get to go out and declare to a lost world. So as you stand and as we're about to respond in song, I want to charge you with this. You can all stand as we respond in song. I want to charge you with this.

Do not fear. Don't fear rejection. The Spirit of God subdues every opposition to the gospel. Don't underestimate what Christ might do in very ordinary people. the gospel advances not because we're sufficient, but because the risen and ascended king is still building his church and not even the gates of hell will prevail against her. Let's pray.

Father, we thank you just for everything that you're doing in our young people, in this church, in all of us, Lord. And I pray today for those who don't know you, who might not share the same enthusiasm about the gospel as I do, or as we do. Lord, I pray that you would minister to their hearts, that the good news of Jesus Christ would be presented to them in a way that compels them to believe, that captures their hearts and minds, and brings them into conformity and to submission to what you would have for them. And Lord, I pray for those who do know you, who do trust you, who do believe in you by faith, would you increase all of our faith to believe in your precious promises. We thank you for saving us, for giving us a story to tell, the story of your Son, our Savior. In his name we pray. Amen.

Paul said this because they were searching for satisfaction everywhere but in Christ. Paul even says they were deserting Jesus for another gospel. Church, don't make the same mistake. Don't make the same mistake. Draw near to Christ and he will draw nearer and nearer to you. He is gentle and lowly and he wants nothing more than to bear our burdens. He delights in welcoming in the repentant. Those who are downcast and depressed among us, those who are weary in their wanderings.

Even now, the risen Christ, he's praying for you. He's praying for exactly what it is that you need.

And if this is his delight, to do all of this for us, to endlessly provide for every one of our needs, then why should we not delight in him?

It's a weird thing to preach on faithful preaching.

But preaching was never meant to be an end in itself. Preaching will stop in the new heavens and new earth. Because we will see Jesus face to face. And that will be the best sermon on any day. the goal of faithful preaching will always be for us to see and savor our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To treasure him and to walk away transformed by him.

So church, let's make every Sunday, every Sunday we come, no matter who's preaching, no matter what the topic is, let's make it an opportunity to receive his word with joy and gladness. Because remember, when Christ is faithfully proclaimed, we will either receive him or reject him, but the gospel is always unstoppable. Let's pray.

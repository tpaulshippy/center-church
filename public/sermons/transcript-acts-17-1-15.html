You're not like the gods of old that people fashioned with their hands. They made mouths. They made ears. They made eyes. But they could not speak. They could not see. They could not hear. You speak. You hear. You see.

And so, Lord, we know that Your Word is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword. We know that Your Word is breathed out by You, Lord. And I pray that we would be a people who get so close to Your Scripture that we feel the very breath of God.

I pray that You would help us to be tuned in to Your truths and Your realities. I pray that we wouldn't fall into the trap of thinking that our life situation is so complex that the Bible can't speak to it. It's not true. But I pray that we would humble ourselves and open our ears and listen. Listen to Your truth that rings forth from every page. Listen to Your voice. I pray that we would have ears to hear. I pray that You would help us to be a people who see Jesus on every page and see His guidance and His direction and His clarity for our lives, Lord. I pray that we would not just know principally and doctrinally that Your Word is living and active. I pray that we would know it experientially.

I pray that we would be aware, aware of the fact that Your Word speaks.

And may we listen.

Thank You that we don't have to grope in the darkness trying to find our way by ourselves. We would surely be lost.

But Lord, You have spoken.

And You have spoken preeminently through Your Son, Jesus Christ.

We can come to You and experience forgiveness. We can come to You and experience comfort. We can come to You and have consolation. And Lord, I pray specifically for those here this morning who do not know You, Lord, I pray that You would help them to recognize that it was necessary for Jesus to die and rise for them. Maybe they don't understand all of what that means, Lord, but I pray that You would by Your Spirit touch their hearts so that they would recognize there's something wrong. there's something wrong with them and that is sin. And so, Lord, I pray that You would help them to turn to You and hope in You and look to You and ask from You forgiveness.

And in Jesus, it's in Your mighty name we pray. Amen.

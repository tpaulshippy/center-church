I pray that you would help us to be a people to give ourselves completely to you because we don't want to hedge our bets. We don't want to use you like a tool. We don't want to use you like some kind of magic incantation and just come to you when we need help, Lord.

We want to be yours heart and soul with no reserve.

And so, Lord, I pray that you would do that work in our lives so that the word of God would indeed prevail. in our lives.

Thank you that you are merciful and gracious, patient and abounding in steadfast love and so we who fail, we who fall, we who have so many flaws, we can turn to you knowing that we have an advocate in heaven, Jesus Christ the Conqueror who is no tyrant but a loving Savior.

And we pray that the word would prevail not just in our midst but in our community as well. We pray that we would see days where the word of God marches forth overcoming the ignorance, overcoming the hard-heartedness, overcoming the darkness of the world around us, Lord. I pray that the light of the gospel of Jesus Christ would shine in such a way so that people would be compelled to respond to you because one day the word will prevail and everybody will see you, Jesus. But we pray before that day, we pray that we would experience those times of refreshing like they had here in Ephesus where the Spirit of God fell and the fear of the Lord came upon everyone and everyone recognized that it was no good just to play at religion.

But Lord, they needed you just as we do and I pray we would respond in just the same way. And it's in your mighty name we pray, Jesus. Amen. Thank you.

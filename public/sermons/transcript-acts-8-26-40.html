An impresario is someone who works behind the scenes to discover, organize, and promote performers that end up in the spotlight. In 1959, at the Redcliffe Motor Speedway in coastal Australia, three unknown brothers sang during the intervals between the motocross motorcycle races. Nobody bought a ticket to hear them. The crowd came for the engines, but not the music. But in the midst of the dust and noise, under the grandstand, these three brothers sang. And it turns out, at least one person was listening. Robert Stigwood, almost an anonymous music impresario, heard something the crowd missed. And in time, he helped turn the Gibb brothers into the Bee Gees, one of the best-selling music groups in all of history. The brothers became famous. Stigwood stayed largely in the background.

That's what an impresario does. Works in the shadows so that someone else might step into the spotlight. A far greater example of an impresario is how God the Holy Spirit works.

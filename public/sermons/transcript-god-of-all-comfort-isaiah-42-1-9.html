We meet Him in the pages of Scripture from Genesis all the way to Revelation. And we find, as we meet Him in the Scriptures, that there is no one like Him.

In Jesus, we meet with this person, this figure that has a paradox of characteristics that are found in no one else. We find Him being infinite in majesty, but yet all-surpassing in humility. We find Him as King Most High, who is also the servant of all. We find Him as the mighty conqueror, who is also meek and mild. We see Him as the prince of peace and a man of war. We see Him as lion and lamb, friend of sinners and enemy of self-righteousness. We see Him with unlimited strength and being drawn to the weak.

And most paradoxically, we see Him as both God and man. No other person, no other person brings together these seemingly contradictory elements. No other person is like Jesus, the Christ.

When we see Jesus, and we see Him in the Scriptures, and we meet Jesus, we give cause to wonder, worship, and follow Him.

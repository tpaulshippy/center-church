May we build all that we are, all that we have, everything that we might become upon him and not forget him, to push him to the side, to push him to the outside, to pretend like he doesn't matter because, friends, of all the things in your life, the thing that matters most today is your God.

May we forget all but remember him. Let's pray. Let's pray.

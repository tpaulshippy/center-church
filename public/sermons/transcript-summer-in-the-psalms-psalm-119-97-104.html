Well, it's because the word translated law in the Old Testament has a much broader meaning than the one used in the New Testament.

The Old Testament word for law literally means instruction.

It's a word which encompasses all of God's teaching, not just His rules, His commandments or His legal requirements.

The Old Testament word for law encompasses all manner of instruction about God's character and about what He has done and what He will do. It's God's explanation of Himself in words that we can understand.

The full ord definition of God's law is what the psalmist is so giddy about. It is God's instruction, His revelation of Himself that He loves and meditates upon all the day.

But just because our psalmist loves God's law doesn't mean we do.

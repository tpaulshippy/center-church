Sunday Sermon: 1 Timothy 2:1-6

We are called to shine bright the love of God.

In a different book of the Bible, the very last book, in Revelation chapter 1, John records a dramatic picture that he had of the risen Lord. He hears a voice louder than a chorus of trumpets, and then he turned, and the text says, to see the voice that was speaking to me, and on turning, I saw seven golden lampstands. It puts light high so you can see by it. The church at large, and ours in specific, is a lampstand meant to shine the light of the gospel of Jesus Christ from us in all that we do. We are made to shine. We are commissioned to shine. We are commanded to shine. We are called as a people to shine forth the love of God, the love expressed in the gospel of Jesus Christ.

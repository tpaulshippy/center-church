What to expect

Here's what a Sunday looks like

01

Arrive

Our service starts at 10:00 AM — come a little early. The Welcome Team meets you at the front doors, and coffee and pastries are free for first-time guests.

02

Have kids?

We offer classes for children up to fourth grade. Our children join us for our time of singing and are dismissed to their classrooms before the sermon. Check in at the Children's Ministry station on arrival.

03

Singing

Our services start with singing to and about Jesus — hymns to contemporary — using music to focus our hearts on Jesus and sing biblical truths to each other.

04

Preaching

We preach straight through books of the Bible over weeks or months, aiming to see Jesus in all of Scripture and let God's Word overflow into how we live.

Common questions

New here? Start here.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable. Some people dress up, others wear t-shirts. Feel free to be yourself.

What happens during the service?

We gather to hear fresh, relevant teaching from the Bible, sing together led by a band, and respond — some take communion, some go to be prayed for. If you're new, there's no pressure to do anything but observe. We give as a church to create space for you; you're our valued guest.

Where do my kids go?

We offer classes for children up to fourth grade. Our children join us for our time of singing and are dismissed to their classrooms before the sermon. Stop by the Children's Ministry Check-In Station when you arrive.

What do you believe?

We hold a high view of the Bible and take sound doctrine seriously. Start with our beliefs page — and ask anyone on a Sunday; we love talking about Jesus.

How can I get involved?

Meet people by joining a small group, or fill out a connect card on Sunday to learn about joining a team. We'd love to help you take a next step.
